TallyTroops Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

TallyTroops ("the game") is made by AKLabs7. This policy explains what information the game uses and why. Contact: ak7labs@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, settings) is saved only on your device. We do not run accounts or servers, and we do not receive this information. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising

The game shows ads using Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares: your device's IP address (which can indicate approximate location), interactions with ads and the app (such as app launches, taps and video views), diagnostic information (such as app performance), and device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID). This data is sent encrypted. Google's use of this data is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Notifications

If you allow reminders, the game schedules notifications on your device (for example, when your lives are full). No data is sent to us for this.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
